About Hunger's Hunt

Discover what we're about and why we built this platform.

What is Hunger's Hunt?

Hunger's Hunt is a restaurant discovery platform that helps you choose a location, explore restaurants nearby, and find your next meal with confidence.

How discovery works

The application combines a curated Hunger's Hunt catalogue with live place discovery. The curated catalogue currently highlights restaurants in Peshawar, while live discovery currently uses OpenStreetMap-based data for other locations.

This is an early version of the project, so some live places may have incomplete images, ratings, phone numbers, or descriptions depending on the available source data. We are actively improving the restaurant information, interface, and overall experience as Hunger's Hunt evolves.

Live place classifications come from external mapping data, so a venue may occasionally appear as a restaurant or cafe even when people would describe it as a hotel, food stall, market venue, or another type of place. Curated Hunger's Hunt entries are maintained separately, and we continue refining how external information is presented.

OpenStreetMap currently powers live discovery. Foursquare remains part of the provider architecture for a future switch-back when its service access is available again.

Why did we build this?

We noticed that finding a good restaurant often means scrolling through endless reviews, checking multiple websites, and still feeling unsure. We wanted to create a single, clean place where you can:

  • Discover restaurants by category, cuisine, or location.
  • See ratings for service and taste/price.
  • View photos, descriptions, and contact details.
  • Get directions with a single click.

What you can discover

Our platform currently highlights restaurants across several categories. As we grow, we aim to cover more cuisines and regions. You can explore:

  • Featured restaurants: Hand-picked spots we think you'll love.
  • Popular choices: Restaurants that have received high ratings from users.
  • Categories: Filter by Fast Food, Café, Pakistani, Chinese, and more.

Our mission

Our mission is simple: connect food lovers with great restaurants through a clean, intuitive experience. We're constantly working to improve and add more features, including user reviews and direct feedback.

Important disclaimer

Restaurant information, such as hours, menus, and contact details, may change over time. We recommend verifying any important details directly with the restaurant before you visit.
